Calcite planner steps

Each query is planned by Planner.plan with detailed_steps. Every step shows the plan after it, as a graph (rows flow upwards) and as text. The rule-based steps also show the plan after each rule that changed it; the operator that the rule produced has a red border. Topology: worker localhost:8181 → localhost:8282 (workers are named by their gRPC address), the sink on localhost:8282; events has one physical source (on localhost:8181), events2 two (one on each worker).
