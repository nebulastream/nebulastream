Latency trace

Pipeline graph

Mean time per buffer spent at each node, over all buffers and over the slowest 1%. Colour shows the share of the tail. Click a node or a dashed network edge to focus the stage charts on it.

Latency over time

One point per buffer (its worst tuple), coloured by the stage it spent most time in. Drag to select a time range, which filters every other chart; click a point for its timeline.

Counters

Engine-wide values sampled every millisecond and recorded when they change, on the latency chart's time axis. Not specific to the selected query.

Breakdown by percentile

Mean time per stage of the buffers around each percentile. Not a sum of per-stage percentiles.

What dominates the tail

For each buffer at or above p99: the stage it spent most time in, counted.

Stage distributions

CDF of each stage's duration over all buffers (solid) and over the slowest 1% (dashed). A stage that moves right in the tail drives it.

Click a point in the latency chart.

Open spans

Spans that began but had not ended when the trace ended, oldest first: a stuck pipeline, or a source waiting for backpressure, a buffer or data.
